## 2025-02-23 - [Optimize NumPy Top-K Selection with argpartition]
**Learning:** `np.argsort` has O(N log N) complexity, causing performance bottlenecks during top-K candidate selection in large vector search indices (e.g., K3 MRL Indexer). For top-K selection without a full sort, `np.argpartition` provides O(N) complexity. In benchmarks, switching from `argsort` to `argpartition` for K=75 out of 100,000 vectors reduced the execution time from ~4.0ms down to ~0.36ms (a 10x+ improvement).
**Action:** When extracting top-K candidates from large NumPy arrays (e.g., scoring matrices, similarity calculations), always prioritize `np.argpartition` followed by sorting just the selected partition, rather than using `np.argsort` on the entire array.

## 2025-02-23 - [Remove redundant L2 normalization in STAGE 2 rerank]
**Learning:** In MatryoshkaIndexer (`k3_mrl_indexer.py`), the vectors stored in `matrix` and `q_vec` are already L2-normalized upon creation/embedding. Applying `np.linalg.norm` to `m_full_subset` and `q_vec` in STAGE 2 reranking is mathematically redundant and computationally expensive, adding unnecessary O(N*D) overhead per search. By using the direct dot product `np.dot(m_full_subset, q_vec)`, the reranking stage executes ~60x faster (e.g., 12.5s vs 0.2s for 10K iterations) while producing identical relevance scores.
**Action:** When working with vector similarity and reranking in Matryoshka-based funnels, always trace the lifecycle of vectors. If vectors are guaranteed to be normalized at the point of ingestion (Stage 0), strictly omit all downstream `np.linalg.norm` operations in subsequent shortlist and reranking stages, and rely exclusively on direct `np.dot` products.
