## 2025-02-23 - [Optimize NumPy Top-K Selection with argpartition]
**Learning:** `np.argsort` has O(N log N) complexity, causing performance bottlenecks during top-K candidate selection in large vector search indices (e.g., K3 MRL Indexer). For top-K selection without a full sort, `np.argpartition` provides O(N) complexity. In benchmarks, switching from `argsort` to `argpartition` for K=75 out of 100,000 vectors reduced the execution time from ~4.0ms down to ~0.36ms (a 10x+ improvement).
**Action:** When extracting top-K candidates from large NumPy arrays (e.g., scoring matrices, similarity calculations), always prioritize `np.argpartition` followed by sorting just the selected partition, rather than using `np.argsort` on the entire array.

## 2025-02-24 - [Optimize NumPy L2 Norm Calculation with einsum and dot]
**Learning:** `np.linalg.norm(A, axis=1, keepdims=True)` on 2D matrices is computationally expensive due to internal overhead and array copies. Replacing it with 1D squared norms using `np.einsum('ij,ij->i', A, A)` followed by square root and new axis addition (`np.sqrt(...)[:, np.newaxis]`) preserves broadcasting shape and yields a ~3-4x speedup. Similarly, replacing `np.linalg.norm(v)` on 1D vectors with `np.sqrt(np.dot(v, v))` avoids the overhead of the linalg function, giving a ~1.6x speedup.
**Action:** When calculating L2 norms for cosine similarities in performance-critical loops (especially over large numbers of vector embeddings), replace `np.linalg.norm` with `np.sqrt(np.einsum('ij,ij->i', A, A))[:, np.newaxis]` for 2D matrices and `np.sqrt(np.dot(v, v))` for 1D vectors.
