## 2025-02-24 - [Optimize NumPy Cosine Similarity Normalization]
**Learning:** Allocating an intermediate normalized N x D matrix (`A / np.linalg.norm(A)`) inside hot loops has a massive allocation cost compared to raw dot product scaled by 1D norms (`np.dot(A, B) / (|A| * |B|)`). When calculating cosine similarity across a large number of vectors during reranking, scaling the raw dot product with cached or dynamically calculated 1D norms avoids expensive matrix allocations and provides a significant speedup (e.g., ~1.5x+ faster for reranking).
**Action:** When calculating dot product or cosine similarity with a large matrix, avoid fully normalizing the entire N x D matrix if it isn't strictly necessary for a cached structure. Instead, compute the raw dot product and scale the resulting 1D score vector by the pre-calculated 1D norms of the matrix and query.

## 2025-02-23 - [Optimize NumPy Top-K Selection with argpartition]
**Learning:** `np.argsort` has O(N log N) complexity, causing performance bottlenecks during top-K candidate selection in large vector search indices (e.g., K3 MRL Indexer). For top-K selection without a full sort, `np.argpartition` provides O(N) complexity. In benchmarks, switching from `argsort` to `argpartition` for K=75 out of 100,000 vectors reduced the execution time from ~4.0ms down to ~0.36ms (a 10x+ improvement).
**Action:** When extracting top-K candidates from large NumPy arrays (e.g., scoring matrices, similarity calculations), always prioritize `np.argpartition` followed by sorting just the selected partition, rather than using `np.argsort` on the entire array.
