## 2025-02-23 - [Optimize NumPy Top-K Selection with argpartition]
**Learning:** `np.argsort` has O(N log N) complexity, causing performance bottlenecks during top-K candidate selection in large vector search indices (e.g., K3 MRL Indexer). For top-K selection without a full sort, `np.argpartition` provides O(N) complexity. In benchmarks, switching from `argsort` to `argpartition` for K=75 out of 100,000 vectors reduced the execution time from ~4.0ms down to ~0.36ms (a 10x+ improvement).
**Action:** When extracting top-K candidates from large NumPy arrays (e.g., scoring matrices, similarity calculations), always prioritize `np.argpartition` followed by sorting just the selected partition, rather than using `np.argsort` on the entire array.
## 2025-05-23 - Optimizing Stage 2 Rerank Normalization in MRL Indexer
**Learning:** In the high-res rerank stage (Stage 2) of the `MatryoshkaIndexer`, calculating the full normalized subset matrix `m_full_subset / (np.linalg.norm(m_full_subset, axis=1, keepdims=True) + 1e-9)` is computationally expensive and forces memory allocation for the intermediate matrix. Since this happens on every query for the top candidates, it's a hot path. Benchmarking shows computing the raw dot product and scaling by 1D squared norms (`np.einsum`) provides a ~2.3x speedup for the reranking stage.
**Action:** Replaced intermediate NxD matrix normalization with raw dot products scaled by 1D squared norms (`np.einsum('ij,ij->i', A, A)`) to significantly reduce latency during the reranking step without breaking mathematical equivalency.
