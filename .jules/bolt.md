## 2025-02-23 - [Optimize NumPy Top-K Selection with argpartition]
**Learning:** `np.argsort` has O(N log N) complexity, causing performance bottlenecks during top-K candidate selection in large vector search indices (e.g., K3 MRL Indexer). For top-K selection without a full sort, `np.argpartition` provides O(N) complexity. In benchmarks, switching from `argsort` to `argpartition` for K=75 out of 100,000 vectors reduced the execution time from ~4.0ms down to ~0.36ms (a 10x+ improvement).
**Action:** When extracting top-K candidates from large NumPy arrays (e.g., scoring matrices, similarity calculations), always prioritize `np.argpartition` followed by sorting just the selected partition, rather than using `np.argsort` on the entire array.

## 2025-02-23 - [Optimize Cosine Similarity with Raw Dot Products and 1D Norms]
**Learning:** For Stage 2 high-res reranking cosine similarity calculations in large arrays, computing a fully normalized intermediate N x D matrix (e.g., `A / np.linalg.norm(A)`) causes a severe performance bottleneck due to large memory allocation. Benchmarking shows computing the raw dot product first and scaling by 1D squared norms (`np.einsum('ij,ij->i', A, A)`) provides a ~4x speedup (e.g., from 1.40ms down to 0.36ms for 1000 candidates of 768 dimensions).
**Action:** When computing cosine similarity for multiple vectors in NumPy, avoid allocating intermediate normalized Nx D matrices. Instead, use raw dot products scaled by 1D squared norms computed via `np.einsum('ij,ij->i', A, A)` instead of `np.linalg.norm`.
